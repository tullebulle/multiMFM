# Training details for TABASCO property-guidance experiments

This document describes the molecular models used for the QM9 property-guidance
and GEOM-Drugs fingerprint-guidance results in Section 3.3 of the dMFM paper.
Settings were recovered from the selected checkpoints, training code, saved
run configurations, and experiment notes. The base generator and the meta flow
map (MFM) have separate training schedules. In particular, the step number in a
selected MFM checkpoint refers to its most recent training phase.

## Molecular representation and base model

We trained unconditional TABASCO-based models jointly over three-dimensional
coordinates and atom identities, using heavy atoms for both datasets. Atom
identities were represented by continuous vectors during the probability path:
Gaussian noise was linearly interpolated toward a one-hot atom-type endpoint.
The model consumed these vectors through a linear projection and predicted
atom-type logits, whose softmax defined the predicted endpoint on the simplex.
Coordinates followed a centered Gaussian-to-data linear interpolant.

The base objective combined coordinate endpoint mean-squared error and atom-type
cross-entropy, with modality coefficients 1.0 and 0.1, respectively. Both terms
used the implementation's inverse-time weighting. Training times were sampled
from a Beta(1.8, 1) distribution. Padding was masked when computing the losses.

## Meta flow map

For each dataset, the base generator was frozen and used to construct GLASS
posterior-velocity targets. The MFM used the corresponding TABASCO backbone,
augmented with the inner times and the outer conditioning state and time.
Both selected students used additive conditioning and endpoint
parameterization. Their hidden widths, layer counts, and head counts matched
their respective teachers.

MFM optimization used Adam with betas (0.9, 0.999), zero weight decay, gradient
norm clipping at 1.0, and linear learning-rate warmup followed by cosine decay.
Selected MFM checkpoints contain EMA weights. The frozen QM9 MFM was reused
across all six property-guidance tasks.

### QM9: generated-condition diagonal refinement

The selected QM9 MFM was refined to match the instantaneous GLASS posterior
velocity on states generated by the teacher. Outer conditioning states were
sampled from unguided teacher trajectories using a 128-step grid. Inner states
were obtained by integrating the GLASS posterior from noise to a sampled inner
time, using a nominal resolution of 16 steps over the full inner interval.
Thus, the actual number of steps to an inner time `s` was
`max(1, round(16 * s))` when `s > 0`.

The refinement objective combined velocity MSE, velocity-direction matching,
and finite-difference matching of sensitivity to the conditioning state.
Coordinate and atom contributions had weights 8.0 and 0.5. The direction term
had coefficient 5.0; the condition-sensitivity MSE and direction terms had
coefficients 0.1 and 0.25. The finite-difference perturbation scale was 0.03.
The diagonal MSE was evaluated in velocity space, without the `(1-s)^2`
endpoint-space weighting.

The final refinement phase used the diagonal objective described above. Although
its saved argument dictionary also contains ESD and rollout settings, those
objectives were not active in this phase.

The initialization checkpoint came from earlier training that matched an
eight-step diagonal Euler rollout against GLASS-64 targets on generated
conditioning states, with a local diagonal anchor and condition-sensitivity
regularization. The experiment notes identify the selected ancestor as the
spread-4 run at step 3,000. The full earlier QM9 training chain has not yet been
reconstructed from surviving checkpoints. Consequently, **1,000 is the number
of selected final-refinement updates, not the total MFM training budget**.

### GEOM-Drugs: diagonal distillation followed by ESD

GEOM-Drugs distillation used a configured subset of 150,000 training conformers;
149,990 survived preprocessing in the training log. The student was initialized
from the EMA teacher and trained in four consecutive phases. Each subsequent
phase loaded the preceding selected student weights and started a new optimizer
and learning-rate schedule.

| Phase | Objective | Selected updates in phase | Schedule horizon | Peak LR | Batch/GPU | GPUs | Global batch | EMA |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | Diagonal GLASS distillation | 20,000 | 40,000 | 2e-4 | 256 | 3 | 768 | 0.999 |
| 2 | Diagonal GLASS distillation continuation | 40,000 | 40,000 | 1e-3 | 256 | 3 | 768 | 0.999 |
| 3 | ESD consistency with diagonal anchor | 20,000 | 30,000 | 1e-4 | 128 | 3 | 384 | 0.995 |
| 4 | ESD continuation with a smaller jump cap | 10,000 | 30,000 | 1e-4 | 96 | 4 | 384 | 0.995 |

All four phases used 500 warmup steps and maximum training time 0.95. The
selected model therefore accumulated **90,000 MFM updates after teacher
initialization**. Schedule horizons are listed separately because the selected
checkpoint was sometimes taken before the end of a phase's cosine schedule.

The first two phases used diagonal velocity MSE weighted by `(1-s)^2`.
Conditioning times were uniform on [0, 0.95]. The ESD phases combined
`2 * diagonal_loss + ESD_loss`, with unit coordinate and atom weights and no
endpoint-space weighting for the diagonal loss. Their conditioning time was
sampled as `0.95 * U^2`, with `U` uniform on [0, 1], and then set to zero with
probability 0.1.

## Connection to the reported guidance experiments

At inference, QM9 used four diagonal MFM integration steps and 32 posterior
samples to estimate the guidance value gradient, within a 128-step outer
sampling trajectory. The corresponding property evaluations used 1,000
generated molecules per property.

The GEOM-Drugs result matching the paper used the phase-4 step-10,000 student,
10 MFM integration steps, four posterior samples, and 100 outer sampling steps.
The saved 64-molecule evaluation reports Tanimoto similarity 0.2271617 and
PoseBusters validity 0.875. These are inference and evaluation settings and do
not contribute to the training-step counts above.
